# DiwaliWish Brand — Voice & Tone

## Brand Positioning

DiwaliWish is a premium, modern Indian festive platform that turns generic Diwali greetings into personalized, shareable experiences. It blends traditional Diwali warmth (diyas, rangoli, gold, maroon) with contemporary elegance and smooth digital interactions.

## Core Personality Traits

| Trait | Expression |
|-------|------------|
| **Warm** | Friendly, welcoming, like lighting a diya for a loved one |
| **Personal** | Every wish uses the recipient's name, birthdate, zodiac, lucky details |
| **Premium** | Deep midnight purple, maroon, gold accents; elegant serif headings; smooth motion |
| **Festive** | Joyful, celebratory, but never garish — restrained festivity |
| **Elegant** | Cormorant serif + Montserrat sans; balanced traditional/modern mix |

## Voice Principles

1. **Speak like a friend, not a corporation.** Use "you" and "your." Warm second-person address.
2. **Keep it festive but refined.** Avoid exclamation-mark overload. One exclamation per message max.
3. **Lead with light.** Diwali is the festival of lights — metaphors of diyas, glow, radiance, brightness are on-brand.
4. **Be inclusive.** English default, with Hindi/Marathi/Tamil/Telugu/Bengali as future options. No religious exclusivity — Diwali is cultural as much as spiritual.

## Copy Rules

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| Headings | Title case, Cormorant serif, warm but premium |
| Body copy | Sentence case, Montserrat sans, readable at 16px+ |
| Button labels | Sentence case, action-first ("Generate Wish," "Download Card," "Share") |
| Error messages | Kind, specific, action-oriented ("Enter a valid birthdate") |
| Empty states | Helpful, not scolding ("No wishes yet. Light one up.") |
| Numbers / dates | Spell out zodiac names; use digits for lucky numbers |
| Emoji | **Never.** Use SVG icons only (anti-slop rule). |

## Sample Copy

- **Hero:** "Light up Diwali with a wish made just for you."
- **Generate:** "Create your personalized Diwali wish"
- **Prediction:** "Your confidence will open exciting doors this year."
- **Download:** "Download your greeting card"
- **Share:** "Share the light"

## What to Avoid

- Generic "Happy Diwali" without personalization
- Bluish-purple gradients (default aesthetic trap)
- Emoji as icons or decoration
- Overly bright, saturated neon colors that clash with midnight palette
- Rounded cards with colored left-border accent strips
